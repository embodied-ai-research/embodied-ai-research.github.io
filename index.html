About简介

I am a researcher at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), where I am a member of the AIR Lab (Autonomous and Intelligent Robot Laboratory), advised by Prof. Jiahu Qin and Prof. Qingchen Liu. I work on embodied AI for robot manipulation, studying how robots can acquire reliable visuomotor skills: learning to see, plan, and act in contact-rich, uncertain environments without giving up safety.

My earlier work centered on safe learning-based control. I now bring these foundations to learned manipulation policies, including brain-inspired vision-action models for efficient visuomotor control.

我目前在中国科学技术大学自主与智能机器人实验室（AIR Lab，Autonomous and Intelligent Robot Laboratory）从事研究工作，师从秦家虎教授和刘卿尘教授。我的研究方向是面向机器人操作的具身智能，关注机器人如何获得可靠的视觉运动技能： 在接触丰富、充满不确定性的环境中学会感知、规划与执行，同时保证安全。

我早期的工作聚焦于基于学习的安全控制。目前，我正将这些基础延伸到可学习的操作策略中，包括面向高效视觉运动控制的类脑视觉-动作模型。

ELogGen

Open source开源项目Data generation for robot learning机器人学习数据生成

ELogGen is an open-source framework for generating diverse robot-learning data from a single demonstration, with a focus on long-horizon bimanual manipulation. It varies operation order, semantic role bindings, and effector assignments while preserving the final task goal.

Feasibility-aware trajectory generation turns these execution plans into robot motions across varied spatial configurations, producing multi-level, temporally aligned context alongside the demonstrations. The repository provides category-packing and drawer-storage examples, environment setup, and command-line tools for generating and validating data.

ELogGen 是面向长时序双臂操作的开源数据生成框架，从单条示范出发生成多样化的机器人学习数据。它在保持最终任务目标一致的前提下，改变操作顺序、物体的语义角色以及左右臂的任务分工，扩展可用的执行方式。

框架结合考虑可行性的轨迹生成，将执行方案转化为不同空间配置下的机器人运动，同时生成多层次、时间对齐的上下文信息。仓库提供分类装箱和抽屉收纳示例、环境配置，以及数据生成和验证的命令行工具。
